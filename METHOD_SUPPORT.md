## Research-only entries (opt-in only)

`FM.moirai2` (CC-BY-NC-4.0 weights), `FM.tabpfn_ts` (TabPFN non-commercial/non-production weights), `FM.timesfm3`
and `EXT.pop` (CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 code) are flagged `research_only`. They are never part of a default profile
(`core` or `all`); naming them requires `xferbench run --allow-research-only` (otherwise exit 2), and such runs
carry `usage_label: internal_research_only` in `run_manifest.json`.

## Licence-pinned donor-prior composites

`FM.chronos2`, `FM.chronos_bolt`, `FM.timesfm25`, `M6.deepar_count`: experimental, donor_only composites, labelled
"licence-pinned, not Reviewer-recommended for PAIR0". They need the `foundation` / `timesfm` / `neural` extras
(not installed or tested here; real backend calls documented-only) and are listed as not_installed until then.
